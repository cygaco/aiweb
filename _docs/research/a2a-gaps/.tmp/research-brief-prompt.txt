8. Contrarian — strongest arguments against the bet: agents will self-build at runtime as models get cheaper; protocol churn (4 breaking revisions in 16 months); platform risk (Anthropic/Google/Apple absorbing primitives); demand side routes around platforms (models read docs directly).
